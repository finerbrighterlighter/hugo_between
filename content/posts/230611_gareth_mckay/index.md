CEB-RAMA-MU hosted a visiting scholar and a frequent collaborator of the department, Dr. Gareth J Mckay from Queen's University Belfast. <!--more--> Dr. McKay has co-authored over thirty papers with different working groups of CEB since 2011.

During his two weeks visit, he shared his experience and expertise on studies regarding genomics and epigenetics. We shared our progress and future plans regarding phenomics of non-communicable diseases using real world data.
