{{- /* One hover label for the IELTS chart: a small box drawn above the thing it describes, hidden until its group is hovered. SVG offers no way to measure text at build time, so the box width is estimated from the character count at a per-size ratio and then clamped inside the viewBox, which is why a long label near the right-hand edge slides left rather than being cut off. The estimate is deliberately generous: a box slightly too wide reads as padding, one too narrow reads as a bug. Expects: text, x (what it points at), y (its bottom edge), W (viewBox width), fs (font size), ch (width per character), h (box height). */ -}} {{- $w := add (mul (strings.RuneCount .text) .ch) 14 -}} {{- $x := math.Max 2 (math.Min (sub .W (add $w 2)) (sub .x (div $w 2))) -}} {{- $y := sub .y .h -}} {{ .text }}
